DATA & PRIVACY

Your data stays yours.

Velora stores your financial data locally in this browser. Create a backup before switching devices or clearing browser data.

Backup your data

Export transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, recurring expenses, goals and settings to one portable JSON file.

Restore a backup

Import a Velora backup from this or another device. Your current local data will be replaced after confirmation.

Export transactions

Export your transaction history as a CSV file for Excel, Numbers, Google Sheets, or your own records.

Delete all local data

Permanently remove Velora data from this browser. Export a backup first. This cannot be undone.

Backup files are not uploaded anywhere by Velora. Keep your backup in a safe place because it contains your financial records.
